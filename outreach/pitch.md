## The one-liner

> Your fans follow what you make. Tuned lets them follow what you're **into** —
> a live page of what you're watching, reading and listening to, with zero effort:
> tap Share → Tuned from any app. No captions, no posting, full veto.

## The 30-second pitch (voice notes / calls)

You already flick links at your audience — Discord dumps, community-tab drops, "5 things I'm
reading." Tuned turns that exact habit into a permanent, beautiful page your fans can follow.
You share from your phone's share menu like you'd share to a group chat; we fetch the title,
thumbnail, everything. Fans see "what you're paying attention to right now," a weekly breakdown,
and the archive. Anything you regret is one tap to hide. It's the lowest-effort channel you'll
ever run, because the content is the attention you're already paying.

## Objection handling (know these cold)

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "I don't have time for another platform" | It's not a platform you post to — it's your share button. If you have time to send a link to a friend, you have time for Tuned. |
| "What if I share something embarrassing / off-brand?" (the caught-liking fear) | Nothing leaves your control: hide any item instantly, and you choose what to share in the first place — this is opt-in per item, not a feed of your history. [When category rules ship: "make whole categories private by default."] |
| "What's in it for me?" | A new fan-engagement surface at ~zero effort; your taste becomes a reason to check daily, between uploads. Early: shape the product; your feed is a flagship. |
| "Why is it free? What's the catch?" | Pilot phase — we need proof fans want this before charging anyone. No lock-in: your data is yours, export/delete anytime, RSS is open. |
| "Who sees my email list?" | Fans who follow leave an email with **you** (your feed) — we don't market to them, and sending doesn't even exist yet. |
| "Is my sharing history tracked?" | We only ever see what you explicitly share. No account linking, no history scraping in the pilot. |

## Pilot terms (say them up front)

- 2 weeks, 2–3 creators, free.
- Ask: try to share when you'd naturally send a link to a friend — no quota, but the habit is the test.
- One 15-minute feedback chat at the end (or voice notes).
- We measure: your publish frequency, fan visits/returns, follows. You see everything we see.
- Exit anytime; feed deletable on request.

## What to send when they say yes

1. Their **studio URL** (mint via `POST /api/creators` — see repo README).
2. One line: "This link is your login — don't share it. Fan link is on the page."
3. Point at **📲 One-tap sharing** in the studio for share-menu setup.
4. The fan link, for their bio/Discord once they've published 3–5 items.
