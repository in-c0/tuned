# Pilot creator shortlist — criteria, archetypes, tracker

**Goal: 1–3 pilot creators saying yes.** Not Bellinghams — mid-tier niche creators who
answer their own DMs and already share links with their audience.

## Selection criteria (all four, or don't bother)

1. **20–200K followers** — big enough that fan-demand is testable, small enough to reach.
2. **Already link-dumps** — a Discord #links channel, community-tab drops, a "5 things"
   newsletter section, "what I'm reading" tweets. We replace a habit, we don't create one.
3. **Low caught-liking risk** — their niche tolerates eclectic taste (a chess streamer sharing
   a math video is charming; an image-managed celeb sharing anything is a PR meeting).
4. **Reachable** — answers DMs / has a public email / shares a community with Ava.

**Order of attack: Ava's own network first.** A warm yes from a 5K-follower friend beats three
weeks of cold-DMing 100K strangers — pilot #1 is for learning, not reach. Korean dev/tech
community and sports-science circles (SSMT adjacency) are the two warmest ponds.

## Archetypes (in rough order of fit)

| Archetype | Why they fit | Where the link-dump already happens |
|---|---|---|
| Indie game dev with devlog audience | Fans explicitly follow their *process and influences* | Discord, itch/Steam updates |
| AI/ML explainer (YouTube/X, mid-tier) | Attention IS their product; audience wants their reading list | "papers I read this week" posts |
| Football/F1 tactics channel | Analysis fans crave sources; low embarrassment floor | Community tab, patron Discord |
| Chess/speedrun streamer | Eclectic taste is part of the persona | Stream chat link spam |
| Sports-science practitioner | Ava's SSMT world; evidence-sharing is the culture | Newsletter link sections |
| Korean tech YouTuber / dev blogger | Ava's bilingual edge; less competition for the pitch | 카페/오픈카톡 link culture |

Concrete names: pick together with Ava per archetype and **verify current follower counts and
DM-openness before contact** — do not trust remembered numbers. (Deliberately not pre-filling
names from model memory; stale facts here are worse than blanks.)

## What would kill the pilot (watch for these)

- Creator publishes 10 things on day 1, nothing after → novelty, not habit. The share-menu
  setup on their phone is the single highest-leverage onboarding step; walk them through it live.
- Fans visit once and never return → "right now" isn't refreshing often enough to be worth
  checking; consider follow-by-email sending earlier than planned.
- Creator asks "can I schedule posts?" → they're treating it as a content channel; gently
  re-anchor: it's a share button, not a calendar.
