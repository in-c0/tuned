# Pilot creator shortlist — criteria, archetypes, tracker

**Goal: 1–3 pilot creators saying yes.** Not Bellinghams — mid-tier niche creators who
answer their own DMs and already share links with their audience.

## Selection criteria (all four, or don't bother)

1. **20–200K followers** — big enough that fan-demand is testable, small enough to reach.
2. **Already link-dumps** — a Discord #links channel, community-tab drops, a "5 things"
   newsletter section, "what I'm reading" tweets. We replace a habit, we don't create one.
3. **Low caught-liking risk** — their niche tolerates eclectic taste (a chess streamer sharing
   a math video is charming; an image-managed celeb sharing anything is a PR meeting).
4. **Reachable** — answers DMs / has a public email / shares a community with Ava.

**Order of attack (owner 2026-07-28: personal network is off the table — cold/public only).**
Niches, in owner's words: *tech solopreneurs; health-conscious people; science lovers; sports
athletes and practitioners.* Cold outreach ranking below reflects fit + reachability, and
@avakim build-in-public posts double as inbound for the solopreneur niche (they ARE that audience).

## What would kill the pilot (watch for these)

- Creator publishes 10 things on day 1, nothing after → novelty, not habit. The share-menu
  setup on their phone is the single highest-leverage onboarding step; walk them through it live.
- Fans visit once and never return → "right now" isn't refreshing often enough to be worth
  checking; consider follow-by-email sending earlier than planned.
- Creator asks "can I schedule posts?" → they're treating it as a content channel; gently
  re-anchor: it's a share button, not a calendar.
